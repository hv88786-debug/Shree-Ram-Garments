## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the regal elegance of Rajasthani heritage couture translated into a contemporary high-end e-commerce experience. The aesthetic fuses time-honored artisanal tradition with the crisp clarity of international luxury retail.

### Visual Character
- **Royal Rajasthani Heritage:** Majestic deep maroons, warm temple stone ivories, and champagne gold filigree evoke the architectural grace of Shekhawati havelis and courtly textiles.
- **Modern Luxury E-Commerce:** High clarity, generous breathing room, razor-sharp hairline borders, and unfussy typography ensure seamless purchasing confidence across desktop and mobile devices.
- **Warmth and Trust:** Designed for families celebrating weddings, festivals, and milestones, conveying hospitality (*Padharo Mhare Desh*), authenticity, and artisanal pride.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in royal tones drawn from dyed silks, zari embroidery, and sandstone palaces.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#700F1C` / Deep Maroon):** Used for top notification banners, brand headers, primary call-to-actions, category accent titles, and focus states. Represents ceremonial majesty and festive abundance.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059` / Champagne Gold):** Applied to delicate hairline dividers, badge borders, star ratings, hover highlights, and decorative accents.
- **Tertiary (`#FAF7F2` / Warm Cream Ivory):** The foundational page background tone. Replaces sterile cool white with a tactile, silk-and-parchment warmth. Secondary surface containers use `#F5EFEB`.
- **Neutral (`#261A1A` / Dark Espresso Charcoal):** High-contrast text color that avoids harsh pure black while guaranteeing WCAG AAA legibility against cream backgrounds. Secondary metadata uses `#66554F`.
- **WhatsApp Accent (`#25D366`):** Dedicated conversion color for direct-to-artisan WhatsApp consultations, floating seamlessly in the lower viewport.

## Typography

The type system blends the editorial gravitas of **Playfair Display** with the pristine digital ergonomics of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Editorial Headings:** Playfair Display delivers classical proportions, echoing royal inscriptions and luxury magazine spreads. It is reserved for section banners, collection titles, and prominent storytelling statements.
- **Operational UI & Commerce Data:** Plus Jakarta Sans drives top bar tickers, navigational links, faceted filters, and product details. Its geometric open counters provide superior legibility when displaying rupee currency (`₹`), size metrics, and fabric specifications.
- **Case Conventions:** Category tabs, primary button actions, and ticker notifications use small tracking-expanded uppercase (`label-sm` and `label-md`) to reinforce an artisanal boutique posture.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured grid accommodates high-density mobile shopping as well as expansive luxury desktop spreads.

### Grid Anatomy
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout, `1rem` margins, `1rem` gutters. Product rails scroll horizontally or snap to a 2-column grid.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` margins, `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout with a maximum container boundary of `1280px` centered within outer margins. Product matrices display 4 cards per row.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Micro Space (`space-xs` to `space-sm`):** Spans between product title, pricing tag, and stock indicators.
- **Component Padding (`space-md` to `space-lg`):** Internal padding for interactive cards, dropdowns, and banner overlays.
- **Section Spacing (`space-xl` to `space-2xl`):** Generous breathing room between curated collection rails, ensuring the imagery commands focus without visual clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on warm tonal layering and delicate gilded hairline contours rather than heavy synthetic drop shadows.

### Depth Stratification
- **Ground (Base):** Flat `#FAF7F2` warm cream canvas representing untreated tussar silk.
- **Surface Level 1 (Card & Category Capsules):** `#FFFFFF` or `#F5EFEB` with a `1px` continuous border in `#E8DECE` or pale champagne gold (`#C5A059` at 25% opacity).
- **Surface Level 2 (Hover States & Modals):** Ultra-soft tinted shadow (`0 8px 24px -4px rgba(112, 15, 28, 0.08)`), imparting an ambient, warm maroon-tinged lift.
- **Floating Overlays:** The persistent WhatsApp concierge button and quick-view actions float at `z-index: 50` with a smooth elevation (`0 6px 18px rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.3)`).
- **Dividers & Borders:** Precision 1px hairline rules tinted with `#D4AF37` at 20-30% opacity separate content modules, maintaining a refined architectural structure.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, tailored geometry (`roundedness: 1`). Subtly softened corners (4px base, 8px on larger panels) convey traditional bespoke tailoring, avoiding both industrial brutalism and generic hyper-rounded playful tech motifs.

### Shape Roles
- **Product Tiles & Hero Cards:** Soft 4px to 6px radii that preserve the crisp, clean silhouettes of ethnic photography.
- **Category Avatars:** Perfect circular shapes (`rounded-full`) framed with delicate gold borders, evoking miniature royal portraits.
- **Action Buttons & Badges:** Subtle 4px corner rounding on primary conversion buttons, with pill options reserved solely for floating WhatsApp triggers and filter pills.

## Components

### 1. Navigation & Utility Bar
- **Announcement Strip:** Full-width `#700F1C` bar with centered `#FAF7F2` tracking-spaced text (`label-sm`).
- **Main Header:** Centered royal brand wordmark in Playfair Display with symmetric icon anchors (Search and Shopping Bag). Below it, clean horizontal text links in Plus Jakarta Sans with subtle maroon underline transitions on hover.

### 2. Category Story Circles
- **Composition:** Perfectly circular portrait containers (72px mobile, 96px desktop) displaying hero garment details against parchment-toned studio backgrounds.
- **Border Treatment:** A 1px ring in `#C5A059` with a 3px padding offset.
- **Labels:** Centered below each circle using `label-md` in `#261A1A`.

### 3. Product Cards
- **Image Aspect Ratio:** 3:4 portrait ratio with soft 4px border-radius, clean `#F5EFEB` backdrop, and subtle zoom on hover.
- **Metadata Block:** Left-aligned beneath the image. Product title in `body-md` (`#261A1A`), followed by the price in bold `price-display` prefixed with the Indian Rupee symbol (`₹`).
- **Secondary CTA:** Subtle wishlist heart icon placed in the top-right corner of the image with a frosted ivory backdrop.

### 4. Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Button:** `#700F1C` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` border radius, high-density padding (`12px 24px`), accompanied by a delicate directional arrow (`→`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `1px` border in `#700F1C` or `#C5A059`, text in deep maroon.
- **WhatsApp Concierge Button:** Circular 56px floating element in `#25D366` containing the official white chat glyph, positioned in the bottom-right viewport with persistent accessibility across mobile browsing.

### 5. Form Fields & Selection Controls
- **Input Fields:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px` border in `#E8DECE`, changing to a `1px` border in `#700F1C` on focus with no blue browser rings.
- **Swatches:** Rectangular fabric/color swatches with a 2px pale gold border indicator when active.
- **Radio & Checkboxes:** Custom rounded selectors filled with `#700F1C` when active, with gold focus outlines.
